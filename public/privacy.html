Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 15, 2026

AstraAds ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our lead management dashboard (the "Service").

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information that is voluntarily provided through our integrated advertising lead forms (e.g., Facebook Lead Ads and Google Ads) or when you configure page connections. This information includes:

  • Contact Details: Full name, email address, phone number, and any custom form question inputs submitted by prospects.
  • Campaign Metadata: Campaign name, form ID, and platform source (e.g., Google or Facebook).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to:

  • Route lead details to the designated client segment on your dashboard.
  • Enable your sales agents and administrators to contact prospects for bookings.
  • Generate aggregate conversion and platform statistics for your campaigns.

3. Data Retention and Security

All data is stored in a secure local database environment on our hosting servers. We implement standard industry security controls (including SSL encryption for all web requests) to prevent unauthorized access, alteration, or exposure of lead data.

4. Data Deletion Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

If you are a prospect or page administrator and wish to have your lead information permanently deleted from our database, you may request deletion by contacting us at: info@sannainnovations.com.

Administrators can request the removal of specific page connections or database purges at any time. All verified deletion requests will be completed within 48 business hours.

5. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent lead data to third parties. Data is processed exclusively for the purpose of serving your designated business CRM.

6. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page with a revised effective date.
