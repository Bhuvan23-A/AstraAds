The Friction

Why Traditional Advertising Tools Fail Small Businesses

Too Complex to Manage

Meta Ads Manager and Google Ads layouts are built for professional marketing agencies. One wrong button click can waste your entire budget in hours.

Wasted Setup Time

Manually creating creatives, copywriting multiple formats, setting up targeting, and configuring instant lead forms takes hours of manual work.

Disconnected Lead Flow

Leads captured on Meta stay stuck inside the Facebook system. Exporting them manually to CSV results in lost opportunities and slow client follow-ups.

The Solution

Automating Campaign Launch in Three Steps

1

Describe Your Product

Type in your business name, target audience, and product offerings. The AI parses this to formulate client-specific messaging.

2

AI Generates Campaign

AstraAds automatically generates optimized keywords, social captions, headlines, budget allocation, and builds native lead collection forms.

3

Launch Directly to Ads API

Connect your ad accounts via OAuth and click "Launch". The system creates the campaign, publishes it live, and sets up active webhooks to capture leads.

Key Features

Enterprise Ad Infrastructure, Built for Simplicity

Intelligent Copywriter

Creates high-converting captions, headlines, and descriptions designed specifically for Google Search and Meta Social Feeds.

Instant Lead Forms

Builds native Instant Forms directly inside the ad networks. Leads autofill page details on-platform, maximizing conversion rates.

Real-time Webhook Sync

Integrates with Google Webhook and Meta Graph APIs to stream lead captures to the Leads Manager instantly as they occur.
